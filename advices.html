Essential Cycling Advices

Discover useful tips and essential tools
for a better cycling experience.

Essential Cycle Tools :

  • Tire Levers :
  • Mini Pump or CO2 inflator :
  • Patch kit :
  • Saddle Bag :

Small plastic tools to help you pop the tire off the rim if you get a flat.

To get air back into your tire after a repair.

Sticky patches to fix a hole in your tube if you run out of spares.

A small bag that clips under your seat to hold all this stuff.

How to Cycle Safe :

Helmet Always : Protect your head; no exceptions.

Stay Visible : Use flashing lights, even during the day.

Eye Contact : Look at drivers to ensure they see you.

Hand Signals : Use hand signals before turning or stopping.
