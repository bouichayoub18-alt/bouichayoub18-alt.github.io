Cycling Nutrition

Good nutrition helps cyclists perform better
and recover faster.

Balance :

Eat carbohydrates ( rice,pasta,..) to power your legs.

Eat protein (chiken,eggs,..) to fix your muscles after riding.

Eat your recovery meal during the first 30-60 minutes after finiching.

Eat more on “Hard” days and less on “Rest” days.

Meal Examples :

Before Training (2-3 Hours Before)

  • Rice Pudding : With a few nuts or cinnamon for energy.
  • Eggs : Boiled or cooked eggs on whole-wheat toast.
  • Fruit : A banana with a bit of peanut butter.

After Training (Recovery)

  • Protein + Carb : Grilled chicken with brown rice and vegetables.
  • Carbohydrates : Rice, potatoes, or pasta with lean meat sauce or tuna.
  • Snacks : Greek yogurt, milk, or a recovery shake ( Chocolate Milk, ...).
